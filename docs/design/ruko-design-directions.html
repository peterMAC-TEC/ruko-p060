Ruko · design directions

Calm until it matters

Three ways Ruko could look and feel, shown on the four screens people see most: the elder's home, the STOP screen, the Companion, and the family's alert. They share one idea. Everyday screens stay quiet. The whole screen changes only when someone may be in danger.

One thing per screenOne main action, always in the same place, big enough for a shaking thumb.
Words, not iconsEvery button says what it does, in English or Hinglish. Icons only help.
Loud only in dangerRed and full-screen colour are kept for STOP and the family alert, so they read at once.
Easy to readBody text 20px or more, buttons 64px or taller, strong contrast in daylight.

Direction A

Pause

Soft, warm and human. One saffron circle is the whole home screen, a pause button you press when something feels off. A friendly serif for the words that matter, a typeface made for low vision for everything else. Red takes over only for STOP and the family alert.

Direction B

Signal

Bold and impossible to miss, like public signage: whole-screen colour, heavy condensed capitals, no decoration. It reads from across a room and in bright sun. The most distinctive of the three, and the loudest, so it asks the most of the family screens to feel gentle.

Direction C

Ink

Quiet and refined, like a good book. Black on white, large light type and almost no boxes. Red appears only for danger, as ink on white, so a red word on a white page is the loudest thing Ruko ever does. The most elegant and the least obviously friendly, so it depends on very large type to stay easy to read.

Recommendation

A for every day, B's loudness for STOP

  • Use Pause (A) for the elder's home and the Companion. The saffron circle is a single, memorable shape that is also the product's idea: pause, then check.
  • Borrow Signal's full-screen red and giant capitals for STOP and the family alert, so danger looks nothing like the rest of the app.
  • Keep Ink (C) for the family and research screens that adults use sitting down, where a quieter, text-led look reads as trustworthy.
  • Before building: choose one, or mix as above. It changes colours, type and layout on every screen, not what Ruko does.
