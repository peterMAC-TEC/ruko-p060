In short

Ruko is one web app (Next.js on Vercel) with a database (Supabase) and an AI model behind a swappable connector. AI reads and words; plain code decides. To make it fully real, three things plug in: a bank or payment app calling our Ruko Guard API, a way to send alerts (SMS or WhatsApp), and the Android call-screening app on real phones.

The architecture: five boxes

Same five boxes as the deck. Follow one reply through it:

1 · INPUTWhat the person says (browser speech-to-text or typing), their yes/no answers, a screenshot, the calling number, or a payment request.
↓
2 · AI / MODELOnly if the rules can't understand it: the AI reads the meaning and returns a label with a confidence score. Below 0.6, or after 3.2 seconds, it's ignored.
↓
3 · LOGIC (code decides)Rules, the scam-stage tracker, the threat score (17 tactics), Money Lock's payment rules, and Ruko Guard's decision. STOP comes from here, never from the AI.
↓
4 · TOOLSDatabase (Supabase), recorded voices, the number-risk check, family alerts, the evidence log, and the bank API.
↓
5 · OUTPUTA big STOP, a family alert, a paused payment waiting for the code word, and the X-Ray trace that shows why.

Safety net around everything: a guard filter checks every AI line, and if the AI is slow, down or offline, the rule-only path still works.

See it live: ruko-seven.vercel.app/architecture, and press X during the live call for X-Ray.

The AI inside Ruko

There are five places Ruko uses AI. In every one, AI reads or words; code makes the decision.

AI helperWhat it doesWhat it can't do
Reply reader (live call)Understands an unclear reply ("sounds fishy, sir") and labels it: doubt, family, refuse… with a confidence score.Can't pick the caller's next move or call STOP.
Scam check reader (/check)Reads what someone typed, said, or a screenshot, and pulls out warning signs, quoting the words it used.Can raise a warning, never lower a STOP.
CompanionAfter hanging up, words calm next steps in plain English or Hinglish.Can't move money or contact anyone.
Ruko Line assistantAsks an unknown caller who they are and reads the answer.The put-through / message / block decision is code, and the server replays it so it can't be faked.
Role-play caller (the fake officer)Used only to practise. Speaks recorded lines chosen by code; AI only helps understand you.Never asks for real OTPs, PINs, cards, Aadhaar; never names real agencies. A guard filter blocked 22/22 unsafe test lines.

Measured: replies understood: rules alone 75.8%, AI alone 93.9%, hybrid 90.9%. The hybrid is a little below AI alone because the AI is ignored when unsure, which keeps it safe and fast. AI fell back to rules on 5.3% of turns. Zero false STOPs on 20 genuine calls. (Test set written by us: synthetic, and labelled so.)

What to plug in to make it fully real

Listed in order of importance. Tool names are options to evaluate, not choices already made; most need an agreement or a paid plan.

1. A bank or payment app (most important)

  • What: the bank calls Ruko Guard before releasing a risky payment; Ruko answers allow / hold for family / decline.
  • Plug-in: a partner bank, UPI app or payment service provider; later, NPCI-level rails (UPI Circle style delegation).
  • Why it matters: without it, Ruko can't stop a real payment. This is also who pays.

2. Alerts that reach the family anywhere

  • What: the family gets the alert even if the app is closed.
  • Plug-ins: web push notifications; SMS via MSG91 or Twilio (suggested in the brief); WhatsApp Business API; a missed-call or voice alert via Exotel for family members without smartphones.

3. Call-time warnings on real phones

  • What: grade the incoming number before it rings.
  • Plug-ins: build and test the Android app (Android Studio, Play Store); number-reputation data from consented user reports, DoT's Chakshu / Financial Fraud Risk Indicator if access is granted, and caller-ID partners.
  • iPhone allows less; there it stays the "Is this a scam?" check and the bank layer.

4. Better voices and languages

  • Plug-ins: Sarvam AI (Indian languages, speech-to-text and voice), Deepgram or Whisper (speech-to-text), ElevenLabs (voices); for phone-number role-play practice, Vapi or Retell.
  • Why: Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi… and practice calls on a normal phone line, not only in the browser.

5. Running it safely at scale

  • Supabase paid plan (backups, uptime), Vercel paid plan, error monitoring (e.g. Sentry), uptime alerts.
  • Security review and penetration test; a DPDP Act legal review; consent records kept properly.
  • Rotate the AI key after the hackathon; keep secrets only in the hosting settings.

6. Proof

  • The 10-elder field test (already designed), then a bank pilot measuring stopped payments and false alarms.

How Ruko runs once it's live

  1. Setup (calm, once): the adult child installs Ruko on the parent's phone, adds trusted family, chooses a code word (stored only as a hash), turns on the bank link and the call-screening app.
  2. Before the phone rings: the Android app checks the number in under 1.5 seconds. Risky: the ring is silenced, a big "Do not pay anyone on this call" shows, and the family is quietly told.
  3. During the call: the elder can tap "Is this a scam?" and answer five questions or say what they heard. Two warning signs → STOP and "Hang up".
  4. At the money: if they still try to pay, the bank asks Ruko Guard. Unusual payment + recent scam signs → held. The family gets the alert and must enter the code word on their own phone to release it. No answer → it stays held for the cooling-off period.
  5. After: the Companion walks them through next steps; 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in are one tap away. Only signal codes are stored, in a tamper-evident log the bank can audit.

Behind the scenes: the phone app and web app talk to Ruko's server on Vercel; the server reads and writes Supabase; the AI is called only when rules can't understand something; and if anything is down, the safe answer is "pause and call family or 1930", never "all clear".

Don't claim: a bank is connected, real calls are screened, SMS alerts are live, or the Android app runs. Say "built and tested on a practice bank" and "next".

How to say it in 20 seconds

"Ruko is a web app on Vercel with a Supabase database. AI reads unclear replies and screenshots; code makes every decision, so it's predictable and works offline. Today the bank is a practice bank. To go live, a bank calls our Ruko Guard API before a risky payment, alerts go out by SMS or WhatsApp, and the Android app warns before the phone rings."
