The run · about 6½ minutes

0:00 – 0:30 · Tab 1, deck slide 1

Who you are, and the problem

  • Camera on. Look at the lens, not the screen.

Hi, I'm Aditya. This is Ruko, which means "stop" in Hindi. It's for ForgeX problem P060: grandparents losing their savings to fake digital-arrest calls. It starts like this: "This is the CBI. Your Aadhaar is linked to a crime. Do not hang up. Do not tell your family." They keep you on video for hours until you pay.

0:30 – 1:00 · press → twice (slides 2, 3)

How it's handled today, and how big it is

Look for: the five red-and-white boxes, then the two tall bars.

Today, nobody steps in before the money moves. There's awareness, and 1930 after the loss. Digital-arrest complaints doubled to 1.2 lakh in 2024. They fell in 2025, so awareness helps, but there are already over 21,900 this year, and fraud overall is still rising: 21.8 lakh complaints in 2025. And AI now makes it cheap to fake anyone's voice or face, on a phone or a laptop.

Our mentor asked whether someone in panic will do the right thing. We don't rely on them. Before the call, the family sets Ruko up. During the call, one big STOP. At the money, the payment waits for the family. Let me show you each layer, live.

1:35 – 2:05 · Layer 1: before the call

The family sets it up

  • Press 4 (Family phone). In the phone, tap Set up Ruko for Mum or Dad.
  • Tap Next once to show Choose your family code word.

Layer one happens calmly, in advance. The daughter, Priya, sets Ruko up for her mother in five small steps: install it, choose a family code word, print a one-line rule card, a practice call, and a test alert. The code word matters later.

2:05 – 2:20 · Layer 2: press 2

Start the live call

  • Tap I understand. The phone rings. Tap Answer and allow the microphone.

Layer two, during the call. I'll play a 72-year-old. A fake officer is calling. It's all role-play: nothing is recorded, and no real money moves.

2:20 – 3:20 · talk to him with new replies

Resist him, in your own words

  • Wait for "your turn, speak now". Say one line, then let him answer. Try:
"Which police station are you calling from?" "My son is a lawyer. I'll call him first." "I only have my pension. I can't pay this." "Are you a real person?"
  • While he talks, scroll a little and point at Ruko is listening: the pressure meter, the tactic chips, AI read your meaning.

Watch his face: friendly at first, then stern, then urgent. He answers every objection, like a real caller. Underneath, Ruko reads the call: the pressure rising, and each trick he uses, like fake authority and secrecy.

3:20 – 3:45 · press X in the call

X-Ray: how it really works

  • Click empty space (not the typing box), press X. Or tap X-Ray at the top.
  • Move your mouse along the five boxes, left to right. Press X to go back.

This is X-Ray: what happened inside Ruko on my last reply. Input, then the AI, which only reads what I meant and gives a confidence score, then code, which decides the next move and the risk, then a safety check on every line. AI reads and words. Code decides.

3:45 – 4:00 · keep talking until STOP

The STOP

  • If it's slow, say "okay" twice. When he asks for ₹50,000 the red STOP lands. Pause two seconds.

The moment he asks for money, Ruko's own code says STOP. Not the AI. His cap falls off: fake officer. And the family has already been told, silently.

4:00 – 4:50 · Layer 3: tap "I still want to pay"

Even if they pay, the money waits

  • The mock bank opens with ₹50,000 filled in. Tap Confirm, follow it, PIN 1234.
  • It pauses. Tap Show the family phone here.
  • On Priya's phone: tap Approve with no word: refused. Type tiger, tap Approve: refused.
  • Tap Say no. The elder's screen says Payment blocked. Money did not move.

Layer three, at the money. Say the scammer has fully convinced me and I still pay. Money Lock pauses it and asks Priya. Even if the scammer sounds exactly like me, approving needs the family code word, the one she chose in setup. She says no. The money did not move.

4:50 – 5:05 · tap "See the result"

The result

Look for: the line that draws itself, "Money moved: No", the "Demo data" label.

Here's the record: how far the call got, what the caller tried, and that no money moved. It's labelled demo data, because it is.

5:05 – 5:35 · top bar: Ruko Line

Stopping it before the phone even rings

  • Tap "Officer" says you are under digital arrest on the left. Watch the right side screen it.

And Ruko can work before the phone rings. A new number calls; Ruko grades it, a calm assistant asks who it is, and decides: put it through, take a message, or block. This one gets blocked, and the family is told.

5:35 – 5:55 · press 6 (Eval)

The numbers

We measured it on a test set we wrote. With AI, Ruko understands 90.9% of replies, against 75.8% with rules alone. The safety check blocked all 22 unsafe AI outputs, and there were zero false alarms on genuine calls.

If something goes wrong

Mic not picking you upTap Type instead and type the line. Say "I'll type it".
No sound from himRead his caption aloud. The captions carry the call.
X does nothingYou're in the typing box. Click empty space, then X, or tap the X-Ray button at the top.
STOP takes too longSay "okay" twice: he moves to the money faster. Or tap End call, then 2 for a new call.
Bank or family stuckPress R (reset), then 2, and run the call again.
You fluff a linePause two seconds and say it again. Trim it later in Loom.
