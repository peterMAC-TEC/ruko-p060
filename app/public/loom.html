The idea: three layers

Our mentor asked whether a panicking person will take the right action. We don't rely on them. The family sets Ruko up before. During the call, one big STOP. And at the money, the payment waits for the family. Let me show you, live.

If something goes wrong

Mic not picking you upTap Type instead and type the line. Say "I'll type it".
No sound from himRead his caption aloud. The captions carry the call.
X does nothingYou're in the typing box. Click empty space, then X, or tap the X-Ray button at the top.
STOP takes too longSay "okay" twice: he moves to the money faster.
Bank or family stuckPress R (reset), then 2, and run the call again.
You fluff a linePause two seconds and say it again. Trim it later in Loom.
