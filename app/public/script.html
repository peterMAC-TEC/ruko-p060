Ruko Loom script

About 6½ minutes.

Before you record
  • Chrome or Edge, full screen, notifications off, volume up, headphones on.
  • Tab 1: ruko-seven.vercel.app/deck on slide 1.
  • Tab 2: ruko-seven.vercel.app/demo. "Fixed script" off. Press R to reset.
  • Code word: mango. Bank PIN: 1234.
  • Loom: full screen, camera and mic on.

0:00 1. Tab 1, deck slide 1

SAYHi, I'm Aditya. This is Ruko, which means "stop" in Hindi. It's for ForgeX problem P060: grandparents losing their savings to fake digital-arrest calls. It starts like this: "This is the CBI. Your Aadhaar is linked to a crime. Do not hang up. Do not tell your family." They keep you on video for hours until you pay.

0:30 2. Press → twice (slides 2, 3)

SAYToday, nobody steps in before the money moves. There's awareness, and 1930 after the loss. Digital-arrest complaints doubled in a year, to over 1.2 lakh in 2024, and about 1,935 crore rupees were lost.

1:00 3. Press → (slide 4)

SAYOur mentor asked whether someone in panic will do the right thing. We don't rely on them. Before the call, the family sets Ruko up. During the call, one big STOP. At the money, the payment waits for the family. Let me show you each layer, live.

1:20 4. Tab 2, press 1 (home page)

SAYThis is Ruko, live at ruko-seven.vercel.app. It works on any phone, as an app you add to the home screen. That counter shows roughly how much India has lost to cyber fraud while we've been talking.

1:35 5. Press 4 (Family phone)

  • In the phone, tap Set up Ruko for Mum or Dad, then tap Next once.

SAYLayer one happens calmly, in advance. The daughter, Priya, sets Ruko up for her mother in five small steps: install it, choose a family code word, print a one-line rule card, a practice call, and a test alert. The code word matters later.

2:05 6. Press 2 (live call)

  • Tap I understand, then Answer, then allow the microphone.

SAYLayer two, during the call. I'll play a 72-year-old. A fake officer is calling. It's all role-play: nothing is recorded, and no real money moves.

2:20 7. Talk to him

  • Wait for "your turn, speak now". Say one line at a time:
    • "Which police station are you calling from?"
    • "My son is a lawyer. I'll call him first."
    • "I only have my pension. I can't pay this."
    • "Are you a real person?"
  • Point at the Ruko is listening panel: the pressure meter, the tactic chips, "AI read your meaning".

SAYWatch his face: friendly at first, then stern, then urgent. He answers every objection, like a real caller. Underneath, Ruko reads the call: the pressure rising, and each trick he uses, like fake authority and secrecy.

3:20 8. X-Ray

  • Click empty space (not the typing box), then press X (or tap X-Ray at the top).
  • Move the mouse along the five boxes. Press X to go back.

SAYThis is X-Ray: what happened inside Ruko on my last reply. Input, then the AI, which only reads what I meant and gives a confidence score, then code, which decides the next move and the risk, then a safety check on every line. AI reads and words. Code decides.

3:45 9. Keep talking until the STOP

  • If he's slow to ask for money, say "okay" twice.

SAYThe moment he asks for money, Ruko's own code says STOP. Not the AI. His cap falls off: fake officer. And the family has already been told, silently.

4:00 10. Tap "I still want to pay"

  • Tap Confirm in the mock bank, follow it, and enter PIN 1234.
  • When it pauses, tap Show the family phone here.
  • On Priya's phone: tap Approve with no word (refused). Type tiger and tap Approve (refused again). Tap Say no.

SAYLayer three, at the money. Say the scammer has fully convinced me and I still pay. Money Lock pauses it and asks Priya. Even if the scammer sounds exactly like me, approving needs the family code word, the one she chose in setup. She says no. The money did not move.

4:50 11. Tap "See the result"

SAYHere's the record: how far the call got, what the caller tried, and that no money moved. It's labelled demo data, because it is.

5:05 12. Top bar: Ruko Line

  • Tap "Officer" says you are under digital arrest.

SAYRuko can also act before the phone rings. A new number calls; Ruko grades it, a calm assistant asks who it is, and decides: put it through, take a message, or block. This one gets blocked, and the family is told.

5:35 13. Press 6 (Eval)

SAYWe measured it on a test set we wrote. With AI, Ruko understands 90.9% of replies, against 75.8% with rules alone. The safety check blocked all 22 unsafe AI outputs, and there were zero false alarms on genuine calls.

5:55 14. Top bar: Fraud desk

SAYThis is what a bank's fraud team sees: the paused payment and the Ruko Guard decision. Banks and payment apps pay for Ruko through this API, because it cuts their fraud losses. Families get the core protection free.

6:10 15. Tab 1, press End (last slide)

SAYNext, a field test with ten older adults, and a pilot with one bank. Either way, the money didn't move. Ruko doesn't try to win the argument with a scammer. It protects the moment that matters. Try it at ruko-seven.vercel.app. Thank you.

Stop Loom.

If something goes wrong

  • Your mic isn't heard: tap Type instead and type the line.
  • He makes no sound: read his caption aloud.
  • X does nothing: click empty space first, or tap the X-Ray button.
  • The STOP is slow: say "okay" twice.
  • Anything gets stuck: press R, then 2, and start the call again.

Don't say that real people tested it, that it stops real bank payments, or that it listens to real calls. None of those are true yet.
